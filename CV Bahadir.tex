 \NewPart{Brief Research Statement}{}
My research focuses on improving parameter estimation and predictive modelling in complex data settings. With my co-authors, I have developed and studied shrinkage, pretest and penalized estimation strategies for linear, partially linear, quantile and high-dimensional regression models. Recent work extends these ideas to robust estimation, spatial data analysis, functional data analysis and statistical machine learning.

These problems arise in computer science, social sciences, biometrics, finance, network security, social media analysis and industrial applications. They create both opportunities and methodological challenges, including noise accumulation, spurious correlations, heavy computational cost and algorithmic instability. My work addresses these challenges through theory, simulation, real-data analysis and reproducible implementation in R and C++.

I am especially interested in collaborative research that connects rigorous statistical methodology with substantive scientific questions.
