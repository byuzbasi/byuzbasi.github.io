At undergraduate level, I have taught design of experiments, probability, categorical data analysis, mathematical statistics, regression, linear algebra, numerical analysis, multivariate statistics and introductory statistics. My graduate teaching includes statistical methods, machine learning and big data analytics.

From 2022 to 2026, I taught at least five courses per semester at İnönü University, including one course in English. I also taught Applied Discrete Data Analysis (STAT 475/675) in English at Simon Fraser University in spring 2025.

The SPSS presentations and datasets currently available in the course archive are primarily Turkish-language teaching materials.
